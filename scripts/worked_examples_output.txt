3. Replicates needed by a simulation study
Worked example: the SD of the conditional fixation time is 9.80 generations (exact chain), so a standard error of 0.5 generation needs R = (SD / 0.5)^2 = 384.1, that is 385 runs that fix; with P_fix = 1.0000 that is also the number of runs.
A fixation probability of 0.73 (small-family row) estimated with a standard error of 0.01 needs R = p (1 - p) / 0.01^2 = 1,971 runs.
